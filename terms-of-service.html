Terms for using ClipForge

ClipForge is a personal, self-hosted tool with exactly one operator. These terms exist because the platforms it connects to require a link to them — there's no sign-up flow or public offering behind this page.

1Who this covers

These terms are between the operator of this ClipForge instance and no one else. ClipForge is not distributed, sold, or made available to other users. Where these terms say "you," it means the operator.

2What ClipForge does

ClipForge downloads or accepts a video, transcribes it locally, splits it into short vertical clips (either by picking highlights or by cutting the whole video into sequential parts), burns in captions, and — only after the operator reviews and approves a specific clip — publishes it to a connected platform such as YouTube or TikTok.

3Content responsibility

The operator is solely responsible for confirming they hold the rights to any source video before processing or publishing it. ClipForge does not verify ownership or licensing on the operator's behalf.

ClipForge's trending-video suggestions are exactly that — suggestions. Nothing is published automatically; the operator reviews and selects a source video, and separately reviews and approves each individual clip before it's ever sent to a connected platform.

7Termination
