Privacy Policy

How ClipForge handles data

ClipForge is a personal, self-hosted video-clipping tool. This page explains, plainly, what it does with the data that passes through it — because it's required reading for the platform integrations it connects to, not because there's a lot to disclose.

1Scope

ClipForge is operated by a single individual, for that individual's own use. It runs on hardware the operator owns and controls. It is not offered as a public service, has no sign-up, and has no users other than the operator. Where this policy says "you," it means the operator.

2Data processed

ClipForge processes:

  • Source video — files the operator uploads, or YouTube URLs the operator supplies (either pasted directly or picked from a trending-video suggestion the operator reviews and selects).
  • Generated transcripts — produced locally by an on-device speech-to-text model. Audio never leaves the operator's own machine for this step.
  • Clip selection — decided either by simple timestamp logic, or by a language model that also runs locally. No transcript or video content is sent to an external AI provider.
  • OAuth credentials — access and refresh tokens for platforms the operator explicitly connects (currently YouTube and TikTok), obtained directly from that platform's own sign-in flow.

3Third-party platforms

When the operator approves a specific clip for a specific connected platform, ClipForge sends that one video file to that platform's official API to publish it. That's the only outbound transfer of processed content this tool performs. Each platform then handles that content under its own privacy policy:

YouTube — governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
TikTok — governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

Fetching a public trending-videos list (to suggest source material) is a read-only call to YouTube's API and doesn't transmit any of the operator's own content.

4Storage & retention

Everything — source videos, transcripts, rendered clips, and connected-account tokens — is stored locally, in a database and on a filesystem the operator alone controls. None of it sits on infrastructure run by anyone else. Data is kept until the operator deletes it; there's no automatic retention window because there's no reason to impose one on a single-user local tool.

5No sale, sharing, or advertising

ClipForge has no analytics, no ad tracking, and no third-party data broker of any kind. Nothing processed by this tool is sold or shared, beyond the one explicit publish action described in Section 3.

6Your control

The operator can delete any project, clip, or connected platform account at any time from within the application, and can revoke ClipForge's access directly from their Google or TikTok account security settings at any time.

7Minors

ClipForge is a personal tool for its single adult operator and is not directed at, or intended for use by, children.

8Changes

This policy may be updated as ClipForge's functionality changes (for example, when additional publishing platforms are connected). Updates take effect as soon as they're posted here.

9Contact

Reach the operator through the Google or TikTok developer account under which this application is registered.
